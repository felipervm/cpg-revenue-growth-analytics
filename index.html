05 · Customer & channel focus

Not every revenue gap deserves the same response.

Customer D is the clearest account-level underperformance at roughly -8.0% below plan. Channel performance also shows that Convenience and Club are farther below plan than Grocery, despite maintaining stronger contribution margins.

06 · Commercial actions

Protect profitable growth. Stop paying for volume that does not pay back.

What I would do next.

The case closes with actions a Sales / Finance / RGM team could debate and operationalize, rather than ending at descriptive reporting.

01

Reallocate trade spend

Reduce deep-discount events with ROTI below 1.0x and redirect investment toward events with proven incremental gross-profit payback.

02

Test selective pricing

Run controlled price tests on SKUs with competitive headroom and lower assumed elasticity, starting with +2% to +3% scenarios.

03

Build Customer D recovery plan

Separate volume, distribution, promotional and rate drivers before selecting corrective actions with Sales.

04

Standardize post-event reviews

Track lift, incremental gross profit, trade spend, ROTI and post-event demand for every promotion, not only topline sales.

05

Validate elasticity

Replace scenario assumptions with observed price-response estimates before making production pricing decisions.
